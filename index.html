Who We Are

A chemical solution should work inside your process, not just your product spec.

Kaarunya Flux Chemicals, an E-Solutions associate, is a startup established on 21 August 2026 in Chennai by a group of engineering graduates with deep expertise and vast experience in electronics manufacturing. Our manufacturing unit is located at 100/6B2B, GNT Road, Verkadu, Gummidipoondi, Thiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu – 601201. Our primary aim is to supply high-quality assembly materials to solar and EMS companies — presently liquid chemicals such as soldering fluxes, industrial collets and cleaners.

The company is classified as non-government and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Tamil Nadu, India, with an authorised and paid-up share capital of ₹10,00,000. We aspire to serve chemical products manufacturing activities across India, currently supplying major sectors including automotive, aerospace, defence, communication, mobiles, electrical, radiators, solar, electronics and EMS. Our Technical Services department is led by a highly experienced team of experts, and capitalizing on the "Make in India" initiative, we play a significant role in providing top-quality solutions to the country's electronics sector. Our manufacturing units are designed with sophisticated, modern testing facilities that satisfy global standards.
